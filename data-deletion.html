Delete your data

EDSA Runner · user data deletion instructions

Paano burahin: buksan ang game → Settings → Account → pindutin ang "Burahin ang account at cloud data" → BURAHIN. Agad na mabubura ang account mo, ang cloud save at lahat ng score mo sa online ranking.

Delete everything yourself (instant)

  1. Open the game and sign in with the same Google, Facebook or email account.
  2. Go to Settings → Account (or tap the account button on the main menu).
  3. Tap "Burahin ang account at cloud data" and confirm with BURAHIN. You may be asked to sign in again to confirm it's you.

This immediately and permanently deletes:

  • your account (name, email, profile picture link, sign-in method);
  • your cloud save;
  • all your scores on the online ranking.

Progress stored only in your browser stays on your device as a guest save. To remove it too, clear this site's data in your browser settings, or use Settings → Reset Progress in the game.

Signed in with Facebook?

After deleting your data in the game you can also remove the app from your Facebook account: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → select EDSA Runner → Remove. Removing the app on Facebook alone does not delete your game data — use the in-game button above (or contact us).

Can't sign in anymore?

Open an issue at github.com/prismo29/edsa-runner/issues asking for deletion (don't post your email publicly — we will reply and confirm privately). We delete the data within 30 days.
