Privacy Policy

EDSA Runner · last updated October 2026

EDSA Runner is a free, non-commercial hobby game that runs in your web browser. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. This page explains the little data the game uses.

Sa madaling salita: puwede kang maglaro nang hindi naglo-login. Kung mag-login ka, ang nase-save lang ay ang pangalan mo, email, game progress at score — para sa cloud save at online ranking. Hindi namin ito ibinebenta o ibinibigay kaninuman, at puwede mo itong burahin kahit kailan.

1. Playing as a guest (no login)

Your progress (coins, characters, settings, your on-device scores) is stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

2. If you sign in (optional)

You can sign in with Google, Facebook or an email address and password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. When you sign in we receive:

  • a random account ID;
  • your display name, email address and profile picture link from Google or Facebook (or the name and email you type);
  • for email accounts, Firebase stores your password securely — we never see it.

We use this only to keep you signed in and to show your name/picture in the game. From Facebook we ask only for your public profile and email address.

3. Cloud save and online ranking

While signed in, the game stores in Google Cloud Firestore (servers in Singapore):

  • Cloud save — your game progress (coins, characters, upgrades, missions and similar game data), readable only by you;
  • Online ranking — your best score per game mode, with the ranking name and barangay you typed, your distance and the date. This ranking is public: anyone playing the game can see it. Use a nickname if you prefer.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • We don't post anything to your Google or Facebook account.

5. Service providers

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages. Accounts and data are processed by Google Firebase (Firebase privacy information). Signing in with Google or Facebook is also subject to their own privacy policies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete your account and all your cloud data yourself at any time: in the game open Settings → Account → "Burahin ang account at cloud data". See the data deletion instructions for details and other options.

7. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and signing in is optional. Children should ask a parent before signing in. If you believe a child's data was stored, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data. Most of this you can do in the game (Account panel); for anything else contact us below.

9. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/prismo29/edsa-runner/issues (please don't post personal information there — just ask us to contact you).
